# Vinyl, 1:1

Cut on a Cricut Maker 4. Black shapes only. Red in the guide files is alignment, not a cut: set that colour to Ignore in Design Space, or just use the cut files.

- `mat-cut.svg` is both pieces on one mat. The deck is the left 88 mm. The faceplate is the right piece, 74 × 45 mm. The bar in the gap is 10 mm. If it does not measure 10 mm after import, scale the whole sheet until it does.
- `deck-cut.svg` and `faceplate-cut.svg` are the same art, one piece each.
- `deck-guide.svg` and `faceplate-guide.svg` add the holes in red so you can line the vinyl up on the print.

Black vinyl on a light deck. White vinyl if the shell is black. One colour. The platter gets nothing: vinyl on a spinning disc walks off.

Port names sit on the jack end of the faceplate, above the screen. FIELD JOG is on the platter side of the screen. TEMPO is the knob. PLAY, CUE, IN, OUT and REV sit beside those buttons. PHONES is the jack on the fader end. The short mark is the centre of the fader throw.
