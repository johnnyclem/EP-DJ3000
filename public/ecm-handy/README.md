# ECM sleeve

A handheld shell for a Sony ECM-77B. The proportions follow the grey field mic with the orange button: 96 mm long, 18.8 mm across, slot grille, cable out the back. It is not that microphone, and it has no logo.

The ECM-77B head is 5.6 mm across and 12.5 mm long, on a 2 mm cable. The XLR power barrel stays on the cable. It does not fit inside this shell, and it does not need to.

## Assembly

1. Print `body.stl` in grey, butt down, no supports. Print `button.stl` in orange.
2. Feed the cable in through the tip until the capsule stops on the lip.
3. Press the button into the side hole. It is a cap. It does not switch the mic.

The bore is 6.1 mm. One wrap of tape if the head rattles. The front hole is 4.6 mm, so the head cannot fall out.

0.2 mm layers, 4 walls. PLA is fine.
