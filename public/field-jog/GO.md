# Field Jog Go

Handheld. A platter you can scratch, a round screen that does not spin, one fader, four buttons.

The shell is 150 × 108 × 18 mm. An Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra plate is 153.36 × 77.76 × 165 mm. The body is taller than the short side of the plate, because a scratch ring will not fit in the old 74 mm height once the fader is under it. Stand the shell on its long edge: 150 mm along the plate, 18 mm across, 108 mm up. Supports stay in the cavity, not on the face. Use 0.03 mm layers so the horizontal lines disappear. The platter is 72 mm and does lie flat, with room to spare on the short side.

## Face

Engineering grey shell. Black platter. Cream bezel. Black caps. Orange knob, orange fader cap, orange rule, orange index on the ring.

The platter is 72 mm. The hole in the middle is 40.4 mm, and it rides a 39.4 mm hub, so there is about 16 mm of vinyl between the hub and the rim. Three grooves, 0.3 mm deep, give a fingertip something to find. The glass is the 1.28 inch GC9A01. It is fixed in the hub. It does not turn. The pin tab points left, clear of the skirt.

Drop the ring over the hub. Press the cream bezel onto the post. The bezel lip sits in a step on the inner edge of the ring, so the ring cannot lift off and the lip does not rub the vinyl. There is 0.35 mm of air under the ring and 0.5 mm of air around the hub.

The ring has a 52 mm gear on the underside. It turns a 13 mm pinion to the right of the hub. A 6 mm diametric magnet sits in the pinion. The MT6701 is in the keep-out under it, on the floor of the tray. That is the scratch sensor. The center of the platter is the screen, so the magnet cannot live there.

A 0.35 mm groove just inside the vinyl is the seat for a ring of copper tape, on the well floor, for the finger. The tape does not touch the ring.

The encoder is the orange knob at the top right. It is an EC11, or anything with a 6 mm shaft.

The fader is the same pot as the 88 mm deck. 75 × 9 mm body, 60 mm of travel, dual-gang, B103, bottom center. The slot is 62 × 3.2 mm. Two M2 screws pull it up to the lid. They do not come through the face.

Cue and Play are the two larger caps on the left of the fader, stacked. Loop and Rev are the two smaller caps on the right, each with a 3 mm LED above it.

USB-C is the left end. The headphone jack is the right end.

Four M2 screws come up through the tray. The face has no screw heads.

## Print

Grey resin for both halves and the platter. Orange for the knob and the fader cap. Black for the four caps. Cream for the bezel. The pinion can be grey or black.

The platter prints flat, face on the plate. The bezel, the caps, the knob and the pinion share that plate. The shell does not. Stand each half on the long edge.

The lettering is 0.45 mm deep. A toothpick of orange resin in the rule, and one in the index notch on the ring, is the second color.

## What it does not have

No MIDI jacks, no line in or out, no battery bay. USB-C powers it. There is room in the tray, under the right cheek and beside the screen, for a small DAC and the ESP32-S3.
